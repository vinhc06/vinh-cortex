My split combines hybrid hypertrophy, plyometrics, and calisthenics with running. I track strength, running times, and vertical jump as separate benchmarks.

I play soccer, flag football at wide receiver and slot, volleyball, basketball, and pickleball. I also practice MMA, Muay Thai, and Brazilian jiu-jitsu, and spend time snowboarding, rock climbing, and hiking.
